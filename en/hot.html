How is heat calculated?

Each item is first scored by rules: base 50 points; freshness (+25 within 6 hours, +20 within 24 hours, +10 within 72 hours); Official sources +10; T3 sources -15; each topic keyword hit (tool, AI, open source, free, launch, productivity, design, developer, data, case study, guide, tutorial, workflow, automation, assistant) +5, capped at +25; each noise keyword -5, floor -15; total capped at 100.
